# Meta Ads weekly check

About 15 minutes. Compare the last 7 days with the 7 days before.

## 1. Spend

- [ ] Spend is within the owner's approved monthly budget, pro rata.
- [ ] No day above 150% of the expected day. No failed payment.

## 2. Results from the lead store

- [ ] Leads, bookings and sales with source `facebook` or `instagram` (UTMs) or from instant
  forms, without test and spam leads.
- [ ] Cost per lead = spend / those leads. Meta's own "results" count is for trends only.
- [ ] Lead quality from the owner or the lead statuses: real customers, wrong service, out of
  area, no answer. Instant form leads that never answer are a sign to switch to the Higher
  intent form or to website leads.

## 3. Delivery and creative

- [ ] Frequency: under about 3 per 7 days for each ad set. Above that, draft new creative or a
  wider area.
- [ ] Link click-through rate per ad. Draft a replacement for the weakest ad after 2 to 4
  weeks.
- [ ] Ad set status: "Learning limited" is normal on small budgets. Do not edit to fix it.
- [ ] No rejected ads. Read the reason; fix the copy, never argue with a policy that applies.

## 4. Tracking health

- [ ] Events Manager: Browser and Server both received `Lead` (and `Schedule` or `Purchase`).
- [ ] Event match quality has not dropped. Diagnostics shows no new issues.
- [ ] The lead store count and Meta's count are within a sensible range of each other.

## 5. Comments and messages

- [ ] Hide spam and scams. Answer real questions within a day (drafts for approval).
- [ ] Never hide or delete honest complaints. Reply with how to reach the owner.

## Report to the owner

```
Meta Ads, week of <date>
Spent $<x> of $<budget> planned. <n> leads at $<cpl> each (last week <n> at $<cpl>).
Best ad: <the ad>, <n> leads. Frequency <x>.
Lead quality: <n> real, <n> not a fit.
For your approval:
1. <change> - why: <reason> - cost: <$ or none> - expected: <effect>
```

Each change request waits for the owner's yes before anyone applies it.
