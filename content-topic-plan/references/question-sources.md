# Question Sources

Collect questions in the customer's words. Keep the wording; fix only spelling. Strip names,
phone numbers, emails and addresses before the question goes into the plan.

| Source | How to get it | What to keep |
|---|---|---|
| Lead forms | Export the last 6 to 12 months of leads (the `need` field in `launch-lead-capture`) | The question or problem, the service, the town |
| Owner and staff | Ask: "What do customers ask on every job?" Record 10 minutes of answers | The question, and the owner's short answer: it often becomes the post's first lines |
| Call notes | Notes from the phone system or the job system | Repeated questions, objections before booking |
| Business reviews | Read the last 50 | Worries ("I was afraid they would upsell me"), outcomes praised |
| Competitor reviews | Read 1- and 2-star reviews of the top 3 local competitors | What went wrong for customers: a post can answer that worry |
| Google Business Profile | Questions and answers on the profile, where Google still shows them; also the search terms in Performance | Unanswered questions, and the words people searched to find the profile |
| People Also Ask | Search the main service + town; open 3 to 5 PAA boxes | The phrasing, and the follow-up questions that become H2s |
| Autocomplete | Type the service + "how", "why", "cost", "vs", "when" | Real phrasing |
| Search Console | Performance > Queries, last 16 months, filter to queries with "how", "why", "what", "cost", "vs" | Questions the site shows for but answers weakly (position 8 to 30) |
| AI answers | Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode the main questions; note which sites they cite | Questions where no local business is cited: an opening |
| Seasons | The owner's busy and slow months; local weather and events | Topics to publish 4 to 6 weeks ahead |

## Merge and clean

- Merge questions that ask the same thing: "how much is a new water heater" and "water
  heater replacement cost" are one topic.
- Keep the most common phrasing as the topic's title draft.
- Note the source next to each question. A question from leads or calls outranks one from a
  tool, because it comes from people who hire.
