# Topic Scoring

`score = lead value x 3 + proof x 2 + demand x 1 - effort x 1`

Range: -3 to 18. Sort by score. Any topic with proof 0 is not scheduled, whatever its score.

## Lead value (x3)

| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| No link to a service | Links to a small or rare service | Links to a core service; the reader hires in months | Links to a core, profitable service; the reader hires within weeks |

## Proof on hand (x2)

| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nothing first-hand | The owner can tell a story, no photos or numbers | One real job with photos, or one sourced number | Several jobs, photos and a sourced number or approved quote |

## Search demand (x1)

| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| No sign anyone searches it | Shows in autocomplete or People Also Ask | Search Console shows impressions for it | Many impressions, or asked in leads every month |

Small businesses rarely have reliable keyword volumes for local questions. Treat leads and
calls as demand too: a question asked every week by customers scores 3.

## Effort (minus x1)

| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| The owner answered it already; photos exist | 15 minutes with the owner | A short interview and new photos | Needs a site visit, a qualified reviewer (health, legal, money, safety) or customer consent |

## Example

| Topic | Lead | Proof | Demand | Effort | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Water heater leaking from the bottom: repair or replace?" | 3 | 3 | 3 | 0 | 18 |
| "Tankless vs tank for a family of four" | 2 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 9 |
| "History of indoor plumbing" | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 (not scheduled: proof 0) |
