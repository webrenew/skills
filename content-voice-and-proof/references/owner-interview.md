# Owner Interview

Twelve questions or fewer. Ask the five **core** questions first; they are enough to write the
first post. Send the list as one message the owner can answer by voice note, or ask them on a
20-minute call and take notes in their words.

Skip any question that the site, `lib/business.ts`, `lib/business-profile.ts` or the reviews
already answer.

| # | Question | Fills | Core |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who are your best customers? Describe two or three: their home or business, their problem, why they called. | Readers | Yes |
| 2 | What do customers ask you most before they hire you? | Readers, topic list | Yes |
| 3 | What do you do differently from others in your trade? Give a real example from a recent job. | Voice, proof bank | Yes |
| 4 | Send 5 to 10 things you wrote to customers: emails, quotes, review replies, texts. | Voice | Yes |
| 5 | Which jobs from the last six months are you proud of? Do you have photos? | Proof bank | Yes |
| 6 | Which towns and neighborhoods do you serve most? What is true about the homes or businesses there (age, water, soil, weather, permits)? | Places | |
| 7 | What may we say about price? Ranges, "from" prices, or nothing? | Claim limits | |
| 8 | What can you promise about timing, and what can you not promise? | Claim limits | |
| 9 | What licenses, insurance and certifications do you hold? Numbers and issuers, please. | Claim limits, authors | |
| 10 | What is your guarantee, word for word? And your cancellation, refund and deposit terms? | Profile `policies` | |
| 11 | Which words or phrases do you never want on your site? | Voice (`wordsWeAvoid`, or `neverSay` for replies too) | |
| 12 | Who writes or approves posts? Their name, role, years in the trade, and a photo. | Authors | |

## After the interview

- Read the answers back as a short summary and ask the owner to correct it.
- Leave each unanswered field empty and add it to the owner to-do list. Never fill it with a
  guess, and never put placeholder text where a post could quote it.
- Ask for consent in writing before any customer's name, face, home or words appear in a
  post. The `content-case-study` skill has a short consent form.
