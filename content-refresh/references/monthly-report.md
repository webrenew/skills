# Monthly Owner Report

The owner reads it in one minute, on a phone. It answers three questions in this order: did
the blog bring customers, what changed, and what do you need from me?

## Template

```
Blog report: <Month YYYY>, <Business>

Leads and calls from the blog: <N> leads, <N> calls (<last month>: <N> and <N>)
Best post: "<title>": <N> leads, <N> calls

Fixed this month:
• <one line per refresh, merge or new post, in plain words>

Needs you (<total minutes> minutes):
• <task, with the link and the time it takes>

Next month: <N> new posts, <N> case study, <N> refresh
```

## Rules

- Lead with leads and calls. Visits come only when they explain a change ("visits from
  Google fell after the season ended").
- Always show last month next to this month. A number alone means nothing.
- At most three owner tasks. Each says what to do, the link, and the minutes it takes.
- Plain words:

| Do not write | Write |
|---|---|
| organic sessions | visits from Google |
| impressions | times we showed up in Google |
| CTR | share of people who clicked |
| SERP position | place in Google results |
| 308 redirect | the old address now forwards |
| pruned | removed an old post that brought no visits |

- No charts, no tables, no attachments. A link to the full inventory is fine.
- If nothing changed and no leads came in, say so in one line and say what will change next
  month.
