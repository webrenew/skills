# Starting negative keywords for a local service business

Add these as an **account-level negative list** (Tools, Shared library, Negative keyword
lists) before launch, then remove any word that the business actually sells. Use phrase match
(`"word"`) unless a note says otherwise, so the list blocks the word in any search.

Review the list with the owner. "Cheap" blocks bargain hunters for a premium roofer and
blocks good customers for a discount cleaner.

## Jobs and training

```
"job"  "jobs"  "career"  "careers"  "hiring"  "salary"  "wage"  "employment"
"apprentice"  "apprenticeship"  "internship"  "resume"  "indeed"
"course"  "courses"  "class"  "classes"  "training"  "school"  "certification"  "license exam"
```

## Do it yourself and research

```
"diy"  "how to"  "how do i"  "tutorial"  "video"  "youtube"  "manual"  "diagram"
"parts"  "part number"  "replacement part"  "home depot"  "lowes"  "amazon"
"symptoms"  "meaning"  "definition"  "what is"  "reddit"
```

Keep "how much" and "cost": a person who asks the price often hires.

## Free and wrong buyer

```
"free"  "donate"  "donation"  "volunteer"  "grant"  "sample"  "template"
"wholesale"  "supplier"  "manufacturer"  "distributor"
```

Remove `"free"` if the business offers free estimates and wants searches such as "free
roof estimate". Put those searches in their own ad group, with the free estimate in the ad.

## Wrong service and wrong place

- Services the business does not offer, from the owner's list (for a plumber: "septic"
  if not offered, "pool", "well pump").
- Towns and states outside the service area that share a name with a served town.
- Competitor names: the owner decides. Bidding on them is allowed; using their trademark in
  ad text often is not.

## Search terms review, each week

For each search term with spend and no lead, ask: "Could this person ever hire us for this?"

| Answer | Action |
|---|---|
| No | Add a negative (exact match `[term]` for one search, phrase for a pattern) |
| Yes, but the wrong page | Move the keyword to a better ad group, or draft a new landing page |
| Yes | Keep, and wait for more data |

Never add a negative that blocks one of the core keywords. Check with the keywords tab
before you save.
