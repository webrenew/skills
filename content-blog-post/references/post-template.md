# Post Template

An outline with one worked example. Change the shape to fit the question; keep the order of
answer, proof, steps, limits, action.

```
H1: Water heater leaking from the bottom? Here is what it means

[Answer block, 40 to 60 words]
A leak from the bottom of the tank itself almost always means the tank has rusted through, and
it must be replaced. A leak from a valve, pipe or fitting near the bottom is often a repair
that costs much less. Check where the water starts before you decide.

[Experience line + real photo]
Last month in Mount Pleasant we found water under a 1998 tank. The drain valve was dry; the
rust was at the seam (photo below). That tank could not be repaired.
  [Photo: Rusted seam at the base of a 1998 water heater tank, the source of the leak]

H2: How do I find where the leak starts?
[One-sentence answer.] Dry the tank and the floor, then watch for 10 minutes.
1. Turn off the power (electric) or set the gas valve to "pilot" (gas).
2. Dry the base of the tank and the floor with a towel.
3. Look at the drain valve, the pressure relief pipe and the pipes on top.
4. Note where the first drop appears.

H2: Which leaks can be repaired?
[One-sentence answer.] Leaks from valves and fittings, not from the tank wall.
- Drain valve: often a part replacement.
- Pressure relief valve: replace the valve, and check why it opened.
- Pipe fittings on top: water runs down and looks like a bottom leak.

H2: How much does it cost?
[Only from the claim limits.] Repairs start from $149. A replacement is quoted on site,
because the price depends on the tank size, fuel type and access.

H2: When should I call a plumber?
[Honest limit.] Call when the water comes from the tank itself, when you smell gas, or when
the relief valve keeps opening. Do not try to replace a gas water heater yourself: it needs
a permit in most of our service area.

[Close: 2-line summary, then the action]
A wet tank seam means replacement; a wet valve usually means a repair.
If you are not sure which one you have, send us a photo or book a visit.
  [Button: Book a service call]
```

## Section checklist

- The answer block makes sense if it is the only text a reader sees.
- Each H2 is a question the reader would ask next, in their words.
- Each H2 section starts with a one-sentence answer.
- At least one first-hand detail from the proof bank appears in the first 200 words.
- Every price, time and promise is inside the claim limits.
- The service page link sits in the body, where the reader is ready to act.
