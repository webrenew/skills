# Consent Template

A short form in plain words. Send it by email or e-signature, or print it for a signature at
the end of the job. This is a starting point, not legal advice: the business's own lawyer can
change it. Clinics use their HIPAA marketing authorization form instead.

```
Permission to share your project

Harbor Plumbing would like to share the story of the work we did for you, so other people
with the same problem know what to expect.

Please tick what we may use:
[ ] Your first name     [ ] Your initials only     [ ] No name
[ ] Your neighbourhood or town (never your street address)
[ ] Photos of the work at your property (no house numbers, plates or faces)
[ ] Photos that show you
[ ] Your words from our interview, as you said them
[ ] Your online review, quoted word for word

Where we may use it:
[ ] Our website     [ ] Our social media and Google Business Profile
[ ] Our emails      [ ] Printed material

You will see the final text before it goes live. You can ask us to remove it at any time:
email hello@harborplumbing.com, and we will take it off our website within 7 days and stop
any new use.

We did not pay you or give you a discount for this permission.
[ ] Exception (we will say so next to your story): ______________________

Name: ______________________   Signature: ______________________   Date: ____________
```

## Rules

- Keep the signed copy for as long as the content is live, plus one year.
- Record the date and the ticked items in the proof bank entry (`consent: written YYYY-MM-DD`).
- Use only what is ticked, in the places that are ticked.
- On a request to remove: take it off the website within the promised time, remove it from
  scheduled social posts, and update the proof bank.
