# Customer Interview

Five questions, 10 minutes. Ask after the consent form is signed. Write the answers word for
word, or record the call with the customer's permission.

| # | Question | Gives the section |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What was going on before you called us? | The problem, in their words |
| 2 | What worried you most about getting it fixed? | The worry other readers share |
| 3 | What happened when we came out? Anything you did not expect? | What we found, what we did |
| 4 | What is different now? | The result |
| 5 | What would you tell a neighbor with the same problem? | The quote |

## How to ask

- Ask open questions and wait. Do not suggest words ("Would you say we were fast?").
- When the answer is vague, ask for one detail: "What did you see?" "How long had it been
  like that?"
- Do not ask for a rating or a review in the same call. A review request goes through the
  normal process (`launch-speed-to-lead`), sent to every customer the same way.

## After the interview

- Write the quote from the answers as said. Trim with "..." only; never add words.
- Send the customer the final quote and the case study text before publish. Their
  correction wins.
- Note any discount, gift or personal connection, for the disclosure.
